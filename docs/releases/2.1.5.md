# Volta 2.1.5 — release notes draft

- **Maintainer:** Johannes Uth
- **Version:** 2.1.5 (build 26)
- **Platform:** macOS 15 or later; Apple silicon and Intel

## Highlights

- **A crisp menu-bar battery.** The icon is vector-drawn at the display's own pixel density. The battery measures 32 × 15 pt and shows a 12 pt bold level figure with high contrast.
- **Everyday information by default.** The dashboard shows the charge level and time remaining, battery health (maximum capacity, charge cycles, temperature) and a collapsed history chart.
- **Open several sections at once.** Click a section title to expand or collapse it. Your choice is remembered, and the panel always fits on screen without scrolling.
- **Advanced extras are opt-in.** Power flow, per-app energy use and technical battery values (mAh, voltage, current) are off by default and can be enabled under Settings → Dashboard → Advanced.

## Distribution status

This file is prepared for the GitHub release.
