# Volta 2.2.1

- **Maintainer:** Johannes Uth
- **Version:** 2.2.1 (build 28)
- **Platform:** macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel

## Highlights

- **Colors that show your charge.** The menu-bar battery is light green above 50 %, light yellow from 21 to 50 % and light red at 20 % or less. You can switch to your accent color or a monochrome icon under Settings → Menu Bar.
- **Clean, readable number.** The battery is a bit smaller so it sits well next to the clock. The number is one dark color on the colored fill and the light gray empty part, like the macOS battery.
- **A simpler first start.** Three short steps: choose your language, allow open at login and notifications, and see where Volta lives in the menu bar. Volta appears in the menu bar once setup is done.
- **Open at login works.** Volta registers as a real login item and appears as “Volta” with its icon in System Settings → General → Login Items.
- **Appearance and Keep open work.** Automatic, Light and Dark apply immediately, and a pinned dashboard stays open when you switch apps.
- **Energy use in percent.** The dashboard shows how much of the energy each app uses (“31 %”) instead of milliwatts.
- **Simpler settings.** Every tab was redesigned with fewer options. Alerts come down to “battery low” and “charge target reached”.

## Distribution status

Do not publish release assets until the app and installer have Developer ID signatures, Apple notarization has completed, and `Casks/volta.rb` contains the checksum of the final notarized ZIP. The local development `.pkg` is unsigned and the local app is ad-hoc signed.
