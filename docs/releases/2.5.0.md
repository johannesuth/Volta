# Volta 2.5.0

- **Maintainer:** Johannes Uth
- **Version:** 2.5.0 (build 38)
- **Platform:** macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel
- **Download:** `Volta-2.5.0.dmg`

## Highlights

- **Extras.** Optional tools you can install and uninstall in Settings → Extras. The first one, **Stay Awake**, keeps your Mac awake, optionally with the display on or the lid closed, with its own dashboard card and shortcuts (⌃⌥W, ⌃⌥D).
- **Lighter type** and a switch to hide the history chart from Settings → General.
- **Your iPhone's battery.** Connected iPhones and iPads appear in Accessories with their charge level.
- **Everything at hand.** Low Power Mode, App Settings, Battery Settings and Quit in their own card at the bottom, every icon in a clear coloured badge.
- **Clear cards.** Each dashboard section sits on its own softly rounded card with a fine edge.
- **Just the logo, if you like.** A fifth menu-bar style shows only Volta's logo, tinted like the macOS icons.
- **Light on your battery.** With the dashboard closed, Volta now uses almost no CPU (0.02 %), and it needs well under half the memory while the dashboard is open.
- **A real installer.** Volta now ships as a disk image with its own designed window: drag Volta onto Applications, eject, done.
- **Alerts you can preview.** Settings → Alerts shows a live preview of the alert while you change it. Choose the color (by charge level or one of six calm colors) and the style: card, card with glowing edge, or a real macOS notification that also appears in Notification Center. Cards stay for five seconds, then fade out.
- **A clearer history chart.** Charging phases are green, battery phases blue, and the line no longer breaks when macOS paused logging while charging. The range switch (6 h · 24 h · 7 days) is a compact pill control, and English time labels read “5 PM”.
- **A compact, readable dashboard.** The panel is about a fifth shorter and narrower than in 2.3.0, while its text is larger.
- **A calmer menu-bar number.** The number in the battery is a little smaller and lighter, matching the macOS battery.
- **Friendlier first start.** The welcome step shows what Volta does at a glance.
- **Free to use, source available.** Volta is free to download and use. The source code is public to read; copying or redistributing it needs permission. See LICENSE.

## First open

Volta 2.5.0 is not notarized yet. The first time you open it, macOS asks for confirmation: click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to “Volta”. This is needed only once.

## Distribution status

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized (see “First open”). As soon as a Developer ID is available, `Scripts/sign-notarize.sh` signs, notarizes and staples the app and the disk image; then put the new checksum into `Casks/volta.rb` before publishing the Homebrew Cask.
