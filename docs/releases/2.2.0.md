# Volta 2.2.0

- **Maintainer:** Johannes Uth
- **Version:** 2.2.0 (build 27)
- **Platform:** macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel

## Highlights

- **Colors that show your charge.** The menu-bar battery is light green above 50 %, light yellow from 21 to 50 % and light red at 20 % or less. You can switch to your accent color or a monochrome icon under Settings → Menu Bar.
- **Easier to read, a bit smaller.** The battery is about 7 % smaller so it sits better next to the clock. The number is dark where it sits on the colored fill and white on the empty part of the battery, so it stays readable from a distance in light and dark mode.
- **Energy use in percent.** The dashboard shows how much of the energy each app uses (“31 %”) instead of milliwatts.
- **Appearance works.** Automatic, Light and Dark now change Volta's windows right away.
- **Open at login works.** Volta registers as a real login item and appears as “Volta” with its icon in System Settings → General → Login Items. Earlier versions used a launch agent that macOS listed as “open”. That agent is removed on first launch.
- **Keep open works.** With the pin on, the dashboard stays open when you click into another app.
- **Better default dashboard.** Battery health, app energy use and history are on by default. Power flow is an advanced extra.
- **Simpler settings.** Every tab was redesigned with fewer options. Alerts come down to two switches: “battery low” and “charge target reached”. The Accessories tab shows each device with its own level bar.

## Distribution status

Do not publish release assets until the app and installer have Developer ID signatures, Apple notarization has completed, and `Casks/volta.rb` contains the checksum of the final notarized ZIP. The local development `.pkg` is unsigned and the local app is ad-hoc signed.
