# Volta 2.1.4 — release notes draft

- **Maintainer:** Johannes Uth
- **Version:** 2.1.4 (build 25)
- **Platform:** macOS 15 or later; Apple silicon and Intel

## Highlights

- A rebuilt menu-bar icon: vector-drawn at the display's own pixel density, a larger 32 × 15 pt battery and a 12 pt bold level figure with high contrast.
- A less sparse default dashboard: Charge, an opened Power flow section and a collapsed History section are enabled; Battery health, Accessories and App energy use remain off.
- The History chart remains unloaded until its section is opened, keeping the compact dropdown responsive.

## Distribution status

This file is prepared for the GitHub release. Do not publish release assets until the app and installer have Developer ID signatures, Apple notarization has completed, and `Casks/volta.rb` contains the checksum of the final notarized ZIP. The local development `.pkg` is unsigned and the local app is ad-hoc signed.
